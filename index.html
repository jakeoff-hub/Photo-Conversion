    Putting a photo into an existing PDF

    1. Add your photo above, pick JPG or PNG, and download it.
    2. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat, Preview (Mac), or a web editor such as Smallpdf or Sejda.
    3. Use its Add image or Insert image tool and pick the file.

    To attach photos as extra pages instead, pick PDF and merge the result with your document.
