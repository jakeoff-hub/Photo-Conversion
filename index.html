HEIC to PDF

Turn iPhone photos (.heic) into a PDF, or into JPGs you can place in any PDF editor. Everything runs in your browser; your photos are never uploaded.

Pages

    Putting a photo into an existing PDF

    1. Add your .heic photo above and choose Download JPG.
    2. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat, Preview (Mac), or a web editor such as Smallpdf or Sejda.
    3. Use its Add image or Insert image tool and pick the JPG.

    To attach photos as extra pages instead, choose Download PDF and merge it with your document.
